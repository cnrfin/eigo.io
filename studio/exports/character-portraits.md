# Great Britain for Beginners: character portrait prompts

One square portrait per character, all in the same style, so the dialogue circles look like a set. Generate at 1024 × 1024 or larger; the studio crops to a circle, so keep the face centred with space around the head.

## Shared style (add to every prompt)

> Head-and-shoulders portrait photo, square format, face centred, looking towards the camera with a natural, friendly expression. Soft natural daylight, plain warm off-white background, shallow depth of field. Bright, modern, slightly warm colour grade. Realistic, not glamorous. No text, no logos, no brand names on clothing.

## Using the real-person references

The references are a casting guide for you, so you can picture the type of face and energy. **Don't put the names in the image prompt.** Most generators refuse real-person likenesses, and using a recognisable celebrity's face for eigo.io could cause problems with image and personality rights. Use the descriptions instead; they're written to produce an original person with a similar feel.

## Main characters

### Haruka (main character)
**Who:** 26, from Osaka, graphic designer on a working-holiday year in London. Curious, a little nervous at first, loves food and taking photos.
**Reference:** the approachable, girl-next-door warmth of **Kasumi Arimura**.

> A 26-year-old Japanese woman with shoulder-length straight dark brown hair, a soft fringe and a warm, slightly shy smile. Natural make-up. She wears a cream knitted cardigan over a simple striped top, with a thin camera strap over one shoulder. Creative and casual, curious eyes. [shared style]

### Gwen (landlady)
**Who:** in her sixties, Welsh, from Cardiff. Kind, loves to talk, very serious about tea.
**Reference:** the warm, chatty energy of **Ruth Jones**.

> A Welsh woman in her mid-sixties with short, soft grey-blonde hair and reading glasses pushed up on her head. Kind, lively eyes and a big, welcoming smile. She wears a teal cardigan over a floral blouse and holds a mug of tea close to her chest. Homely and warm. [shared style]

### Tom (flatmate)
**Who:** late twenties, from Manchester. Friendly, funny, talks a lot, loves football, works in a bike shop, always late.
**Reference:** the boyish, cheeky friendliness of **Tom Holland**.

> A 28-year-old white British man with messy, short light-brown hair and light stubble. A cheeky, open grin, as if he's just told a joke. He wears a navy hoodie over a plain T-shirt, with a bicycle helmet hanging from one hand. Relaxed and sporty. [shared style]

### Priya (colleague)
**Who:** around 30, from Birmingham, parents from India. Project manager, very organised, loves making plans.
**Reference:** the bright, confident warmth of **Charithra Chandran**.

> A 30-year-old British-Indian woman with long dark hair tied back in a neat low ponytail and small gold earrings. A confident, friendly smile. She wears a smart mustard blazer over a white top and holds a slim notebook and pen. Organised, professional and approachable. [shared style]

### Callum (Tom's friend)
**Who:** late twenties, from Edinburgh, lives in London. Easy-going, funny, proud to be Scottish.
**Reference:** the friendly, down-to-earth humour of **Lewis Capaldi**.

> A 28-year-old Scottish man with short, slightly wavy dark-blond hair and a short, neat beard. A broad, easy-going smile with crinkles around the eyes. He wears a dark green waxed jacket over a grey jumper. Friendly and relaxed, outdoorsy. [shared style]

## Supporting characters

### Kenta (Haruka's younger brother)
**Who:** around 20, university student in Japan. Clever, a bit shy, a big Harry Potter fan.
**Reference:** the gentle, youthful look of **Ryo Yoshizawa** in his early career.

> A 20-year-old Japanese university student with neat black hair, a slightly long fringe and thin round glasses. A shy, gentle smile. He wears a simple dark navy jumper over a white collared shirt and a long knitted scarf in deep red and gold (no logos or crests). Bookish and kind. [shared style]

### Linda (Tom's mum)
**Who:** around 60, from Manchester. Warm, talks fast, calls people "love".
**Reference:** the warm northern-mum energy of **Julie Hesmondhalgh**.

> A 60-year-old woman from the north of England with short, layered auburn hair and a big, warm laugh. Bright eyes and laughter lines. She wears a colourful patterned blouse and holds a party paper cup, as if at a garden barbecue. Chatty and full of energy. [shared style]

**Who:** around 38, lives in Cardiff with her family. She looks like her mum.

## Minor speakers (optional)

These appear once each. You can leave their circles as initials, or use these simpler prompts.

- **Barman (Lesson 3):** A friendly British barman in his forties with a short beard, a black T-shirt and a tea towel over one shoulder, in a warm pub. [shared style]
- **Waitress (Lesson 4):** A cheerful café waitress in her fifties with tied-back hair and a simple apron, holding a notepad. [shared style]
- **Passenger (Lesson 7):** A helpful London commuter in her thirties with a backpack and a wireless headphone around her neck. [shared style]
- **Stranger (Lesson 3):** A young man in a dark coat, looking apologetic, on a city street at night. [shared style]

## Tips for consistency

- Generate Haruka first, then reuse her image as a style reference (image-to-image or "style reference" setting) for the others, so lighting and colour match.
- For Gwen and Sian, generate Gwen first and use her as a reference for Sian, so they look related. Do the same for Haruka, Kenta and Mum.
- Keep each character's clothes the same in every image of them across the course, especially Haruka.
- After uploading, set each character's bubble colour in the studio (Course details → Characters) so the colour suits the picture.
