# Great Britain for Beginners: Watch and describe video prompts

Each video is made in two steps:

1. **Start frame:** generate the image prompt as a wide landscape image (2304 × 998, about 21:9). Add the character portraits as reference images so faces match the dialogue circles.
2. **Animate:** give that image to an image-to-video tool with the video prompt. Export 5–8 seconds as MP4 or WebM, no sound.

The same prompts are in each Watch and describe video block's settings in the studio.

**Start frame prompt**

> A 26-year-old Japanese woman with shoulder-length straight dark brown hair and a soft fringe, wearing a cream knitted cardigan over a striped top stands at the front door of a red-brick London terraced house on a quiet street, holding the handle of a big suitcase, seen from the side so both she and the door are in frame. Realistic photo, wide landscape frame (2304 × 998), bright natural light, warm modern colour grade, like a still from a British TV drama. No text, signs, logos or subtitles.

**Video prompt**

> The woman pulls the suitcase the last step to the door, stops, looks up at the house, takes a slow deep breath and knocks on the door twice. Calm, slightly nervous. Static camera, realistic natural movement, no talking, 6 seconds.
